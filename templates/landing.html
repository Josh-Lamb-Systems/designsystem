FAQs

Questions we hear before every sprint

What do I actually get at the end of the sprint?

Four things: an AI Opportunity Map of your core workflows, a prioritized recommendation list with ROI estimates for each item, a phased implementation roadmap (this week / this month / this quarter), and a specific tool stack with monthly costs. Everything is presented live in a final 60-minute walkthrough, and you keep all the documents.

How much of my team's time does it take?

About 3–5 hours total across two weeks: a short intake questionnaire, a 60-minute kickoff, 30-minute interviews with 2–3 team members, and the 60-minute final presentation. We do the heavy lifting between those touchpoints.

Do you implement the recommendations too?

The sprint delivers the roadmap; implementation is a separate engagement. Many clients have us build their top quick wins right after the sprint, but every recommendation includes the tools, costs, and setup steps so your own team can execute it without us.

We're not a tech company. Will this work for us?

That's exactly who this is for. We work with operations-heavy small businesses (contractors, real estate teams, agencies, professional services) and we prioritize no-code tools. If your team can use Google Sheets, they can run these workflows.

We tried AI before and it didn't stick. Why would this be different?

Most AI projects fail because a tool got picked before the problem was defined. The sprint works in the opposite order: map the workflows first, find the repetitive rule-based tasks, then match tools to those specific gaps. That's also why we can offer the money-back guarantee.

What happens after the sprint?

You choose: implement the roadmap with your own team, have us build the quick wins, or move into a larger systems engagement. There's no obligation either way. The roadmap is yours.
